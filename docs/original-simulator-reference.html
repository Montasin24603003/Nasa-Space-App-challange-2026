Data sources. Radiation baseline: NASA Curiosity RAD (Gale Crater, 0.64 mSv/day). Dose limit: NASA-STD-3001 career limit of 600 mSv. Oxygen (0.84 kg) and water (4.35 kg) per crew member per day: NASA Life Support Baseline Values and Assumptions Document (BVAD). Solar particle events: NASA DONKI catalogue of 2024 episodes, replayed with a 50% chance each to reflect that Mars does not always face the same part of the Sun as Earth. Dust storm shape: published Curiosity and JPL optical depth records. Site values, dose spike multipliers, power budgets, plant sizing and starting reserves are model assumptions, not mission requirements.
